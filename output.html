The Wrap

The morning brief was wrong. The ISM Manufacturing PMI for May came in at 54.0% — the highest reading since May 2022 — against a consensus of 53% and a narrative built on the assumption that goods-sector contraction was the structural headwind behind slow job growth and the case for Fed cuts. That is capital-intensive, productivity-driven growth — exactly the environment where AI capex justifies itself. The ISM is not a threat to the AI cycle. It is the confirmation.

AVGO prints tonight. Every data point today points the same direction. The question is not whether the cycle is real — the ISM answers that. The question is how much of it is priced.
